Financial Impact & Timing of Churn

Quantifying Lost Revenue and Lead-Time Vulnerability

Realized vs. Lost Revenue

  • Total Realized Revenue: €12.04M (96.36% from completed check-outs + 3.64% retained non-refundable deposits).
  • Lost Zero-Revenue Cancellations: €5.32M across 9,450 unfulfilled bookings (23.59% of all bookings).
  • Impact Scale: Zero-revenue cancellations represent 45.83% of total check-out lodging revenue.

Lead-Time Vulnerability

  • Average Lead Time (Canceled): 130.63 days (vs. 78.84 days for completed stays).
  • Timing Concentration: 55.62% of all cancellations occur 3 to 12 months in advance (91–365 days).
  • Actionable Opportunity: Early cancellation windows allow inventory recapture; automated re-confirmation at 60/30 days will mitigate €5.32M in leakage.

Knowledge Inventory & Next Steps

What We Know, What We Do Not Know, and What Data We Need Next

1. What We Know (Empirical Findings)

40,060 bookings analyzed (Jul 2015 – Aug 2017) yielding €12.04M realized revenue. Overall cancellation rate is 27.76% (€5.32M lost). Direct/Corporate channels are highly stable (13–15% cancellation), whereas OTA/Groups are volatile (35–42%). Non-refundable policies fail as a deterrent (94.94% churn).

2. What We Do Not Know (Analytical Gaps)

Root causes of guest cancellations (e.g., flight disruptions, speculative multi-hotel bookings). Whether released inventory from early cancellations (91–365 days out) was successfully resold at equal ADR. Proportion of payment failures vs. guest-initiated cancellations.

3. What Data We Need Next

Post-cancellation guest survey reason codes, daily room inventory availability/occupancy logs to measure true displacement, and regional Algarve competitor ADR/occupancy benchmarking.
